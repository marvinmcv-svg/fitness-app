How it flows

One app for the whole training day.

From the morning check-in to tonight's protein, FuerzaFlow keeps the plan moving with you.

01

Seven questions. Your program.

Tell us your goal, schedule and equipment. FuerzaFlow picks the split, the exercises and your weekly sets per muscle.

  • Full gym, dumbbells or a minimal home setup
  • Swap any exercise for one that suits you
02

Check in. The workout adapts.

Sleep, energy, soreness and the time you have. Ten seconds, and today's session is trimmed or pushed to match.

  • Short on time? Main lifts stay, extras go
  • Sore chest? It still trains, with less volume
03

Log fast. Progress on autopilot.

Tick a set and the rest timer starts. Next time, your weights and rep targets are already set from today.

  • Progression hints on every lift
  • New records called out the moment you hit them
04

Snap your plate. Hit your macros.

Photograph a meal, scan a barcode or search. Daily calories and protein are set from your body and goal.

  • AI estimates each food on the plate
  • Millions of packaged foods by barcode
05

See every muscle on target.

Weekly sets per muscle against your range, activity over three weeks, and a weekly review from your coach.
