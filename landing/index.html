Why Setwise

Most workout apps log what you lifted. Setwise understands how you trained.

Primers, partials, eccentrics and weekly volume targets are built into the app, so you never squeeze them into a notes field.

No caps

Unlimited routines on the free plan

A push/pull/legs split repeated twice is already six routines. Setwise never charges you for having a real program.

Technique-aware

Partials and eccentrics count

Log “+4 partials” on the set where they happened. Primer and warm-up sets stay out of your volume totals.

Sets per muscle

Weekly targets for every muscle

See which muscles are behind before the week ends, so you can fix it on Thursday instead of noticing next month.

Personal from day one

Seven questions. One program that fits.

Tell Setwise your goal, experience, schedule and equipment. It picks Full Body, Upper / Lower or a 6-day split and fills it with exercises you can actually do.

  • Home with dumbbells? Cable moves are swapped for ones that work there.
  • Pick up to 3 focus muscles and each gets an extra set.
  • Swap any exercise for one with the same movement and muscles.

In the gym

Log a set in two taps. Know what to lift next time.

Last session's numbers sit next to every set. Hit the top of your rep range on every set and Setwise suggests the next weight, rounded to the nearest 2.5 kg.

  • Rest timer starts when you tick a set: 60 s for primers, 150 s for heavy work.
  • Heavy lifts add weight after a clean session and deload 10% after three misses in a row.
  • Your screen stays awake while you train, and logging works without signal.

Nutrition

Snap your plate. Get your macros.

Your calorie and protein targets come from your check-in. Take a photo of your meal for an estimate of each food, scan a barcode, or search the food list.

  • Protein, carb and fat rings show grams left for the day.
  • Photo estimates list each food so you can untick anything that's wrong.
  • Barcodes are matched against Open Food Facts.

Progress

See every muscle against its target band.

Bars turn green when a muscle lands inside its weekly range and stay violet while it's behind. Secondary muscles count as half a set, so the numbers stay honest.

  • Targets from 8–14 sets for new lifters to 12–24 for advanced.
  • Sessions, working sets and tonnage for the last 7 days.

Questions

Before you join

Is Setwise free?

Logging, unlimited routines, your history and the weekly muscle counter are free. A Pro plan will add automatic progression, the full planner and deeper analytics.

Which phones does it work on?

Setwise runs in the browser on iPhone and Android, and you can add it to your home screen. Native apps with Apple Health and Health Connect come next.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Workouts are saved on your phone first, so a dead zone in the gym never loses a set.

How accurate are photo estimates?

They're estimates, not lab measurements. You see every food and its grams before saving, and can untick anything that's wrong.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Import from Strong and Hevy CSV exports is on the launch list, so you keep your past numbers.

What happens to my email?

It's used only to send your invite and launch news.
